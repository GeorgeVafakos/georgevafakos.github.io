Computational work

Cold atmospheric plasma modelling

I develop captionFOAM to model the interaction of plasma dynamics, reactive chemistry and gas flow in cold atmospheric plasmas.

  • OpenFOAM / C++
  • Drift–diffusion
  • Plasma chemistry
  • LES / RANS

University of Cyprus · ONISILOS / IgnitePLASMA

captionFOAM source code

Plasma Actuators

Surface dielectric-barrier discharge (DBD) plasma actuators for active aerodynamic flow control. Depending on the power supply, they operate in AC or pulsed mode.

  • Ionic wind
  • Aerodynamic flow control
  • OpenFOAM

Publications

  • G. Vafakos, P. Papadopoulos & P. Svarnas · Journal of Applied Physics · 2025
  • V. Papadimas et al. · Applied Sciences · 2021

AC Voltage

In AC operation, an induced fluid flow is generated by the EHD force.

During my PhD, I developed the three-stage model for efficient multiscale plasma–flow coupling.

Pulsed Voltage

In nanosecond pulsed mode, the short pulse produces negligible induced flow but generates a thermal shock wave that interacts with the boundary layer.

Shock position and speed for 10, 20 and 30 kV pulses.

Jet Flow - Air/Helium Mixing

Modelling of laminar and turbulent helium jets in quiescent atmospheric air. A dynamic LES subgrid model is used for describing the turbulence.

Rotating propeller using the MRF approach

Modelling of rotating components using the Multiple Reference Frame (MRF) approach, on a fixed mesh that couples rotating and stationary flow regions.

NACA 0015 Airfoil

NACA 0015 at 15° angle-of-attack, using steady-state RANS with the Spalart–Allmaras turbulence model, using a C-type structured mesh. The flow is separated at this AoA.

Liquid-metal cooling for fusion reactors

The full MHD equations are solved using the UoPfluid in-house Fortran finite-volume code for fusion-reactor cooling under the influence of strong magnetic fields.

  • CVP method
  • Hunt’s case

About

I’m a research fellow at the University of Cyprus, working on cold atmospheric plasma modelling. I have a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Patras.

My work covers physical models, numerical methods and scientific software, with experimental diagnostics for validation. I develop solvers in OpenFOAM, C++ and Fortran, and use Python for data preparation and analysis.
