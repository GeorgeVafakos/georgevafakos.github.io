Scientific & Technical Skills

Modelling of physical systems

  • Reacting multi-species transport and chemistry: Advection–diffusion–reaction equations with stiff chemical kinetics, detailed and reduced reaction mechanisms, conversion of chemical mechanisms into species balance equations, and pseudo-1D kinetic modelling (ZDPlasKin).
  • Fluid dynamics: Jet and plume dynamics, turbulent mixing with ambient air, buoyant flows, environmental street-canyon flows, LES and RANS turbulence modelling, finite-volume CFD for incompressible and compressible flows with heat transfer, aerodynamic flow control, airfoil lift and drag, rotating flows (propellers), and structured and unstructured mesh generation.
  • Coupled multiphysics: Multi-stage one-way coupled and monolithic models across different time scales, plasma–flow and electrohydrodynamic coupling, magnetohydrodynamic flows for liquid cooling of fusion reactors, and hypersonic re-entry flows with high-temperature non-equilibrium gas–surface chemistry.
  • Numerical methods: Finite-difference and finite-volume discretisation of PDEs, numerical methods for ODEs, discretisation schemes for hyperbolic PDEs and higher-order advection schemes, and pressure–velocity coupling algorithms (SIMPLE, PISO).
  • Low-temperature plasma modelling: Drift–diffusion and three-species models, plasma kinetics and detailed plasma chemistry, plasma jets and AC/ns-pulsed surface DBD actuators, and cross-section data handling (LXCat, BOLSIG+).

Scientific programming and data handling

  • Programming languages: Python (NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib), C++, Fortran 2003, MATLAB, R and C.
  • Scientific software development: Numerical solvers, libraries and boundary conditions in OpenFOAM and stand-alone codes, object-oriented design in C++, Fortran and Python, development and maintenance of open-source research software, version control with Git/GitHub and public code releases.

  • Environment and tools: Linux (primary operating system since 2012), OpenFOAM (expert developer), gmsh, ParaView, LaTeX, BOLSIG+, ZDPlasKin and COMSOL Multiphysics (plasma module).

Research software

CaptionFOAM

OpenFOAM · C++ / Python

Open-source framework for cold atmospheric plasmas, with detailed and reduced chemistry, drift–diffusion models, self-consistent electric fields and custom boundary conditions. Couples to CFD and includes an automated LXCat / BOLSIG+ transport-data pipeline. Validated against gap discharges, surface DBD actuators and helium plasma jets.

UoPfluid

Finite-volume solver · Fortran 2003

Object-oriented 2D/3D solver for incompressible flow and heat transfer, with extensions for conducting flows at low magnetic Reynolds number. Includes lid-driven cavity, channel-flow and backward-facing-step benchmarks.
